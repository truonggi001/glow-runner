# Marketing Plan — Glow Runner

## Positioning
"3D auto-runner where your progress literally lights up the world"

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Casual gamers 16-30 who enjoy Geometry Dash, Subway Surfers, Flappy Bird
- **Secondary:** Score-chasers who like leaderboard competition
- **Bartle types:** Achiever (high score), Daredevil (speed, risk)

## Comparable Titles
| Game | Downloads | Price | Key difference |
|------|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Geometry Dash | 100M+ | $1.99 | 2D rhythm-based, no glow progression |
| Subway Surfers | 1B+ | F2P | 3D runner, no glow transformation |
| Flappy Bird | 50M+ | F2P | 2D one-button, no visual reward |

**USP:** Glow transformation — your visual reward IS your progress meter. World goes from dark to radiant as you survive.

## ASO Strategy (App Store Optimization)
- **Title:** Glow Runner — Neon Dash
- **Keywords:** runner, neon, dash, glow, endless, score, speed, jump, reflex
- **Icon:** Bright green-cyan glowing capsule on dark background
- **Screenshots:** Dark→bright glow progression (3 screenshots showing glow levels)
- **Trailer:** 15s gameplay loop, ends with full glow

## Launch Plan
1. **Soft launch** (Vietnam, Philippines) — 2 weeks, collect telemetry
2. **Iterate** — balance based on D1/D7 retention
3. **Global launch** — Steam + mobile simultaneously
4. **Content update** — new glow skins every 2 weeks (seasonal themes)

## Community
- Discord for score sharing + skin requests
- Twitter: devlog GIFs of glow progression
- Reddit: r/runnergames, r/indiegaming

## Budget
- Marketing: $0 (organic first)
- Influencer outreach: free keys to 5-10 small streamers
- ASO tools: free tier (Sensor Tower, App Annie)
