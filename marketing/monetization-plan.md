# Monetization Plan — Glow Runner

## Model: Free-to-Play (F2P) with Cosmetic IAP

### Why F2P + Cosmetic
- Core loop is score-chasing → high replayability → ad-supported viable
- Pillar 2 (speed=beauty) → cosmetic glow skins are natural monetization
- No pay-to-win (pillar 1: flow over frustration — never punish non-payers)

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | Price | Rationale |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Reward ads | Free | Watch ad → continue run (1 revive per session) |
| Cosmetic skins | $0.99-$2.99 | Glow color themes (fire, ice, rainbow, void) |
| Remove ads | $2.99 | One-time purchase, removes all ads |
| Shard doubler | $1.99 | Permanent 2× shard collection (faster glow, NOT higher score) |

### KPI Targets (soft launch)
| Metric | Target | Method |
|--------|--------|--------|
| D1 retention | ≥35% | Telemetry: % returning next day |
| D7 retention | ≥15% | Telemetry |
| Session length | ≥5 min | Telemetry mean |
| ARPDAU | ≥$0.05 | Revenue / DAU |
| LTV (90 day) | ≥$1.50 | Kaplan-Meier + ARPU |

### Platforms
1. **Steam (PC)** — $2.99 premium (no ads, all cosmetics unlocked)
2. **Mobile (iOS/Android)** — F2P with ads + IAP

### Anti-patterns (CỐ TÌNH không làm)
- No pay-to-win (no score multiplier, no extra lives)
- No energy system (no play limits)
- No loot boxes (cosmetics sold directly, not randomized)
- No aggressive ads (interstitials only on death, opt-in reward ads)
